Build a premium, modern, highly professional e-commerce FRONTEND redesign for the Algerian store **EDENGROUPES**.

IMPORTANT: This is primarily a FRONTEND/UI/UX project. Do not build a real backend, payment system, authentication system, or real order-processing infrastructure at this stage. Use realistic mock product data and interactions where necessary.

## 1. RESEARCH THE EXISTING STORE FIRST

Before designing anything, inspect and analyze the existing EDENGROUPES website:

https://edengroupes.com/

Also inspect this product/tag context:

https://edengroupes.com/product-tag/edengroupes/

Understand the actual product catalog, categories, brands, product naming style, Algerian market context, and existing navigation structure.

Do not simply copy the existing visual design.

The goal is to create a significantly better frontend while preserving the actual business identity and commercial positioning of EDENGROUPES.

## 2. UNDERSTAND THE BUSINESS IDENTITY

EDENGROUPES is an Algerian e-commerce store focused heavily on professional tools, equipment, workshop supplies, and practical products.

Its catalog includes products such as:

* Power tools
* Hand tools
* Welding equipment
* Mechanical tools
* Plumbing tools
* Electrical equipment
* Construction tools
* Carpentry tools
* Painting equipment
* Agricultural and gardening equipment
* Automotive tools and accessories
* Diagnostic/scanner equipment
* Lighting
* Safety shoes and work equipment
* Money counting machines
* Household/practical equipment
* Other useful technical products

Important brands include products from brands such as:

CROWN, INGCO, BEETRO, HONESTPRO, HONESTPLUS, POWERBLU, FIXTOP, DINGQI, MODE PRO and others.

The store is strongly oriented toward practical users and professionals.

The website currently organizes products not only by product category, but also by profession, such as:

* Builder
* Mechanic
* Welder
* Plumber
* Carpenter
* Electrician
* Painter
* Gardening/Agriculture
* Shops and merchants

This "shop by profession" concept is extremely important and should become a strong part of the new UX.

## 4. CORE DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a **Professional Industrial E-commerce** visual language.

Think:

professional workshop + modern industrial catalog + trustworthy Algerian e-commerce.

The interface should feel:

* Solid
* Technical
* Clean
* Structured
* Modern
* High quality
* Efficient
* Confident

Avoid excessive decoration.

Every visual element should have a commercial or usability purpose.

Use strong visual hierarchy and excellent product photography.

The website should feel like a serious established equipment retailer rather than a small generic online shop.

## 5. COLOR SYSTEM

Develop a coherent industrial color system.

Use a predominantly neutral foundation:

* White
* Very light gray
* Charcoal
* Deep graphite

Use a strong industrial accent color inspired by the existing EDENGROUPES identity.

Use the accent color selectively for:

* Primary CTA buttons
* Active navigation
* Important badges
* Price highlights
* Interactive states
* Small visual accents

Do not flood the entire website with the accent color.

The product images should remain visually dominant.

The overall result should be professional and high contrast without becoming aggressive.

## 6. TYPOGRAPHY

The website serves an Algerian audience.

Support Arabic and French properly.

Use a modern highly legible Arabic/Latin font system.

Arabic must not feel like an afterthought.

The interface must support:

* Arabic
* French

Structure the frontend so RTL/LTR can be switched cleanly later.

Product names may remain in their natural French/technical naming style when appropriate.

Do not translate technical product names artificially.

The new website must remain recognizably EDENGROUPES.

Do not transform it into:

* a luxury tools brand
* a minimalist Scandinavian store
* an electronics-only store
* a hardware manufacturer
* a marketplace like Amazon
* a generic dropshipping store

It is an Algerian multi-category professional tools and equipment retailer.

Preserve that identity while dramatically improving:

* visual quality
* information architecture
* product discovery
* trust
* usability
* mobile UX
* conversion flow

## 25. FINAL DESIGN GOAL

When someone opens the homepage, within approximately 3 seconds they should understand:

"EDENGROUPES sells professional tools and practical equipment in Algeria."

Within approximately 10 seconds they should be able to:

* search for a product
* browse by category
* browse by profession
* browse by brand
* discover promotions

The final result should feel like the evolution of an established Algerian professional equipment retailer into a modern, high-quality e-commerce platform.

Do not merely redesign individual sections.

Design a complete, coherent visual system for the entire storefront.

Start by analyzing the existing EDENGROUPES website and then create the frontend.
